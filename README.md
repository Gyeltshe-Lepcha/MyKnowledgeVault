# MyKnowledgeVault

Personal Knowledge Management Platform

## Overview

MyKnowledgeVault is a dynamic platform designed to help individuals organize, store, and interact with their personal knowledge in multiple formats—notes, images, videos, and documents. It combines a personal knowledge vault with AI-powered tools, interactive quizzes, and categorization features to create a highly flexible, smart, and engaging learning environment.

Inspired by the simplicity of Google Drive and Notion, MyKnowledgeVault allows users to securely manage their content while benefiting from AI assistance, learning reinforcement, and intuitive categorization.

## Intended Audience

MyKnowledgeVault is intended for:

* Students and lifelong learners managing personal study material
* Professionals curating project knowledge, documentation, or media
* Individuals who want a single platform for notes, media, and interactive learning

## Key Features

### Knowledge Dashboard

Organize, upload, and manage your knowledge in multiple formats including text, images, and videos with an intuitive dashboard.

### AI Chatbot Assistance

* Get instant answers to your queries
* Summarize your notes or content
* Help with content tagging and categorization
* Support interactive learning and quizzes

### Categories & Tags

Organize content into multiple categories and tags for easy retrieval and structured learning.

### Interactive Quiz System

* Create quizzes from your notes and media
* Track performance and learning progress
* Reinforce knowledge retention with AI-generated questions

### Media & File Support

Upload, view, and manage files of any type including PDFs, images, and videos within your knowledge vault.

### Search & Retrieval

Powerful search functionality to quickly find notes, media, or quiz results across your vault.

## Platform

* Web (Responsive design)
* Mobile (iOS & Android planned)

## Technology Stack

* **Frontend:** Next.js, Tailwind CSS
* **Backend:** Node.js, Express.js
* **Database:** PostgreSQL
* **AI Features:** OpenAI API or other AI service integration
